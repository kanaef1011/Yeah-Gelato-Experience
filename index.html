Stop one · Vietnam

Cốm Dừa & Phở

Cốm dừa folds young, hand-pounded rice into shredded coconut — a street-side treat that shows up every autumn, wrapped in lotus leaf. Alongside it, phở: the slow beef broth and fresh herbs that start a thousand mornings in Vietnam. We steeped both into gelato without losing what made them home in the first place.

Young rice Coconut Star anise broth

Stop two · China

White Rabbit Candy & Sichuan Peppercorn

White Rabbit is the milk candy nearly every Chinese childhood shares — soft, sweet, wrapped in paper you could eat. We paired it with something sharper: Sichuan peppercorn and sea salt, the tingly, savory hit found on street snacks from Chengdu to Chongqing. One flavor for nostalgia, one for a little numbness on the tongue.

Milk candy Mala tingle Sea salt

Stop three · Singapore

Kaya Toast & Hainanese Chicken Rice

Kaya toast is the coconut-pandan jam and butter that opens most Singaporean mornings, best dunked into soft-boiled egg. Chicken rice is the dish everyone's family insists on ordering a specific way — poached chicken over rice cooked in its own fat and stock, chili on the side. Two comfort foods, one island, folded into gelato.

Pandan & coconut Soy egg Ginger & chili
